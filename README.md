I'm Sofus Laubel. I have a master's in computer science from the University of Southern Denmark, and I currently work as an Associate in Research at Duke University with Ingrid Daubechies, on Horizontal Diffusion Maps. I'm interested in machine learning in general.

Some of the projects I have made:

- [HDMaps](https://github.com/MorphMath/HDMaps), a Python library for Horizontal Diffusion Maps, which I co-maintain.
- [Parasol](https://github.com/Portfolio-Solver-Platform/parasol), a portfolio executor for MiniZinc written in Rust, from our master's thesis. Together with the portfolio selector we built on top of it, it got second place (silver) in the Open category of the [MiniZinc Challenge 2026](https://www.minizinc.org/challenge/2026/results/), behind Google's OR-Tools CP-SAT.
- [Kvint](https://github.com/sofusl90/kvint), a two player strategy game with an MCTS AI that I wrote from scratch in Rust. It is free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/dk/app/kvint/id6769683429).
